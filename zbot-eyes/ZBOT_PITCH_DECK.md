**Mental Preparation** (15 min before):
- [ ] Review key metrics (LTV/CAC, market size)
- [ ] Practice demo script
- [ ] Prepare for common objections
- [ ] Warm up voice (speak out loud)

**Buffer**: 3 minutes for Q&A or extended demo

### Vocal Delivery Tips

**Pace**:
- Slow down for key metrics (LTV/CAC, market size)
- Speed up slightly for familiar concepts
- Pause for 2-3 seconds after major points

**Emphasis**:
- **Bold** words should be louder/slower
- Use hand gestures for "3 key features"
- Point to specific parts of slides

**Energy**:
- Start high energy (hook)
- Build excitement during demo
- End with confident ask

### Body Language

**Stance**:
- Stand to the left of screen (audience's right)
- Face audience 80% of time (not screen)
- Use open hand gestures

**Movement**:
- Step forward for key points
- Step back during transitions
- Walk to screen only for demo

**Eye Contact**:
- Scan entire room (don't fixate on one person)
- Hold eye contact for 3-5 seconds per person
- Look at decision-makers during "ask"

## 🛡️ OBJECTION HANDLING SCRIPTS

### Objection 1: "Why not just use drones?"

**Bad Response**: "Drones are different."

**Good Response**:
> "Great question. Drones excel at aerial inspection, but they can't climb walls or access tight spaces. Z-BOT inspects scaffolding interiors, vertical surfaces, and confined areas that drones simply can't reach. Plus, our thermal + RGB fusion provides better defect detection than single-camera drones. We're complementary, not competitive—some customers use both."

**Follow-up**: Show comparison slide or video of Z-BOT in tight space.

---

### Objection 2: "Boston Dynamics already dominates robotics."

**Bad Response**: "We're better than Boston Dynamics."

**Good Response**:
> "Boston Dynamics is incredible at quadruped robots like Spot, but Spot walks on floors—it can't climb walls. More importantly, Spot doesn't have conversational AI. Our ChatGLM integration makes inspection data accessible to non-technical stakeholders, which is critical for compliance. We're solving a different problem in a different market segment."

**Follow-up**: "In fact, we see potential partnership opportunities with Boston Dynamics for multi-robot deployments."

---

### Objection 3: "This seems expensive for construction companies."

**Bad Response**: "It's not that expensive."

**Good Response**:
> "At $45K, Z-BOT costs less than 3 months of manual inspection labor. Our pilot customers saw ROI in 6-8 months. Plus, we offer an Inspection-as-a-Service model at $800-2,000 per inspection for customers who don't want to buy hardware. The real cost is *not* using Z-BOT—safety incidents, delayed projects, and missed defects cost millions."

**Follow-up**: Show ROI calculator or customer testimonial.

---

### Objection 4: "What if ChatGLM API goes down?"

**Bad Response**: "It won't go down."

**Good Response**:
> "Excellent technical question. We have local fallback models on the Raspberry Pi. If the API is unavailable, the robot continues detecting and logging—it just won't generate conversational summaries until connectivity is restored. All data is cached locally and synced when the connection returns. We've tested this extensively in offline environments."

**Follow-up**: "We're also exploring on-device LLM options for fully offline operation."

---

### Objection 5: "Manual labor is cheap in China."

**Bad Response**: "Labor costs are rising."

**Good Response**:
> "That's changing rapidly. Labor costs in China are rising 8-10% annually, and safety regulations are getting much stricter after recent high-profile accidents. More importantly, manual inspection can't provide 24/7 monitoring, predictive analytics, or digital audit trails. We're not replacing workers—we're making inspections safer, faster, and more data-driven. Our customers use Z-BOT to *augment* their teams, not replace them."

**Follow-up**: "Plus, insurance companies are starting to offer premium discounts for automated inspection—10-20% savings."

---

### Objection 6: "How do you prevent competitors from copying you?"

**Bad Response**: "We have patents." (if you don't)

**Good Response**:
> "Great question. Our moat is threefold: First, we have 18 months of head start and real customer data—that's hard to replicate. Second, our ChatGLM integration and prompt engineering is proprietary and continuously improving. Third, we're building network effects through our data platform—the more inspections we do, the better our AI gets. By the time competitors catch up, we'll be two generations ahead."

**Follow-up**: "We're also filing patents on our hybrid climbing mechanism and multi-modal detection algorithms."

## 🎯 SUCCESS METRICS

### Pitch Performance KPIs

**Engagement**:
- Questions asked during pitch (target: 3+)
- Follow-up meeting requests (target: 30%+)
- Business card exchanges (target: 50%+)

**Conversion**:
- Term sheet offers (target: 5-10% of pitches)
- Due diligence requests (target: 20% of pitches)
- Warm introductions (target: 40% of pitches)

**Feedback**:
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) from audience (target: 50+)

### Iteration Process

**After Every 5 Pitches**:
1. Review feedback notes
2. Identify most common objections
3. Update slides to preemptively address
4. Practice new responses
5. A/B test different demo scripts

**Monthly Review**:
- Conversion rate trends
- Time spent on each slide (adjust if needed)
- Demo success rate (live vs. video)
- Investor questions (add FAQ slide if needed)
